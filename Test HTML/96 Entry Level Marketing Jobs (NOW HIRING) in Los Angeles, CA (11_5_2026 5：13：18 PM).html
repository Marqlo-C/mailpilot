Job description

Marketing and Sales Associate (Entry-Level)

Company: Isabella Marketing Solutions

Location: West Hollywood, CA

Schedule: Monday – Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Compensation: Base Pay ($700/week during training, scaling to $1,000/week post-training) + Uncapped Commissions & Performance Bonuses

Job Overview

Ready to transition out of late nights, erratic restaurant shifts, or hospitality gridlock into a stable Monday–Friday career with real upward mobility?

Isabella Marketing Solutions is looking for energetic, people-first individuals based in the West Hollywood area to join our team as an Entry-Level Marketing and Sales Associate. If you have a background in hospitality, retail, restaurants, or hotel management, you already possess the secret sauce we look for: unmatched customer service instincts, grace under pressure, and a natural ability to connect with people.

We provide a complete professional playbook and hands-on leadership training to help you pivot your customer-facing experience into a lucrative corporate marketing and sales career. No prior B2B marketing experience is required—your people skills are your greatest asset.

What You'll Do (Your Day-to-Day)

  • Client Engagement & Marketing: Execute localized marketing and sales strategies for businesses in the West Hollywood and greater Los Angeles area, acting as a trusted brand ambassador.
  • B2B Relationship Building: Present solutions face-to-face to local business owners, driving client acquisition and revenue growth.
  • Daily Strategy & Alignment: Participate in morning team workshops, development huddles, and ongoing coaching sessions to sharpen your skills.
  • Goal Tracking: Track pipeline metrics, hit weekly benchmarks, and contribute to a supportive, high-energy team environment.

Who We're Looking For (The Ideal Background)

  • Industry Experience: Direct experience in hospitality, restaurants, retail, hotel services, or customer-facing environments where multitasking and service excellence are second nature.
  • People Skills: Exceptional communication, emotional intelligence, and a natural charisma that builds immediate rapport.
  • Growth Mindset: Eagerness to learn, coachability, and a desire to build a long-term professional career rather than just work another job.
  • Reliability: Accountability, punctuality, and a strong work ethic.
  • Availability: Full-time availability for an on-site schedule from Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Compensation, Perks & Benefits

  • Guaranteed Base Pay: $700/week during your initial training program, increasing to $1,000/week once training is complete.
  • Performance Incentives: Lucrative commissions and weekly performance bonuses on top of your base pay.
  • Health & Wellness: Comprehensive health programs available to support your well-being.
  • Culture & Advancement: A vibrant, mentorship-driven culture with clear, performance-based merit promotions into leadership.

Equal Opportunity Employer. We celebrate diversity and are committed to building an inclusive environment for all team members. All employment practices comply fully with West Hollywood municipal codes, California state labor laws, and the California Fair Chance Act.

Company Description

Isabella Marketing Solutions is a premier sales firm in the LA area. We provide telecom clients with exceptional customer service and new business development. At the heart of our business we are looking to grant our clients long term customers and new acquisitions that will become lifelong partners. Sales is the vehicle we use, but it is not the only thing we focus on here at IMS.

What makes IMS unique is our training program for our staff. Professional and personal growth mean more to us than making a sale. We provide a fast tracked career plan for our team.
