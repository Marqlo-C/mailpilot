BUILD AN AMAZING CAREER WITH SYSTEM PAVERS!

Job Description


System Pavers is growing at a tremendous rate, and we’re looking to expand our sales team with a motivated, outgoing Outside Sales Consultant. In this position, you’ll meet with homeowners to discuss their outdoor living needs. You’ll then help bring their vision to life by discussing options for their space, including project estimates and personalized design possibilities.

Our Design Consultants Enjoy:


  • Nearly unlimited earning potential. First year performers can make $125k+. Our top performers have been hitting $200k+ in income!
  • W-2 employment status which includes full benefits: health, dental, vision, life, and a 401K with company match.
  • Continuous education and career development, including our SP University and field training.
  • Ample opportunities for financial and career growth in an industry that’s booming.


Job Responsibilities:


  • Make initial contact with clients via company generated appointments and build rapport.
  • Accurately uncover and assess customers’ needs and vision for their outdoor space.
  • Measure outdoor spaces and design personalized solutions.
  • Maintain a homeowner-centric focus that prioritizes service and satisfaction.
  • Work closely with the construction department to provide a seamless customer experience.
  • Utilize company technology platforms for designing projects, submitting contractors, etc.
  • Generate leads in your personal pipeline in tandem with the company leads.

Experience & Skills:


  • Previous sales experience is not required but you must have a strong interest in kickstarting your sales career.
  • Comfortable using basic office programs and learning new technology.
  • Must have your driver’s license and be able to meet driving insurance guidelines.
  • Construction experience a plus.


About You


  • Self-motivated:     You are committed to making the most of every opportunity you're presented with as well as creating your own opportunities. You will leverage every resource available to you and have a desire for continuous learning & development.
  • Competitive: You have a strong desire to win and are driven to outperform others.
  • Personable: You're known for your outgoing personality and professionalism.
  • Construction Knowledge: You have a desire to learn or currently possess a basic understanding of construction principles, including the ability to accurately measure spaces and apply basic math skills.
  • Flexible & Adaptable: You're able to adjust your strategies and behaviors with a willingness to accommodate others.
  • Attention to Detail: You have a keen eye for details and take pride in accuracy.
  • Great Communicator: You excel in both verbal and written communications.
  • Creative Problem Solver: You love the idea of helping homeowners fulfill their outdoor living dreams through projects that add lasting value and beauty.

About Us


As the nation’s leading outdoor remodeling company, System Pavers has been creating gorgeous, personalized living spaces that deliver quality, beauty, and durability we proudly stand behind. Since opening in 1992, more than 90,000 homeowners have trusted us to transform their outdoor lifestyles through remodels that add lasting value to their homes. As an established company with a reputation for stellar work, we’re looking forward to many more years of meeting and exceeding customer expectations as leaders in our industry.


We invite you to experience and become a part of our amazing culture as we help one another pave the way to personal and professional success. Join our energetic and positive team as we help guide homeowners through a rewarding outdoor remodeling journey.


Find out more about us by visiting our website or visiting our careers page, here.

As an Outside Sales Consultant at System Pavers, you will see visible results of your work and everything you do will make a difference to the success of our entire company. System Pavers has a fantastic and dedicated team focused on helping our customer make memories with the friends and family.


System Pavers is proud to be an inclusive and equal opportunity employer of a diverse set of individuals and will consider for employment all qualified applicants. System Pavers requires the satisfactory passing of a standard background check for all positions and a clean driving record when driving for the job is required.
